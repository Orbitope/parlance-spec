*title The Lighthouse Stair
*author Parlance importer fixtures
*scene_list
  startup
  keeper
  ending

*create lamp_lit false
*create courage 50
*create oil 2
*create name "traveller"
*create strength 10

*achievement kept_watch true 10 Kept Watch
  You kept the keeper company.
  You kept the keeper company for a while, and/or a little longer.

*comment the opening scene
The stair winds up inside the tower, one iron step at a time.
Someone has written ${name} on the wall, [i]twice[/i] // in chalk.

*choice
  #Climb quickly.
    *set courage +5
    You take the steps two at a time.
    *goto top
  *selectable_if (oil > 1) #Refill the lamp first.
    *set oil -1
    *set lamp_lit true
    The wick drinks the oil and brightens.
    *goto top
  *hide_reuse #Listen at the wall.
    The wind answers, and nothing else does.
    *goto top
  *if (courage < 20) #Go back down.
    *ending

*label top
*if lamp_lit
  The lamp throws your shadow up the stair ahead of you.
*elseif oil > 0
  You still have oil enough for the way down.
*else
  It is very dark.
*set courage %+10
*if courage > 60
  The top is close now.
  *goto_scene ending
You have ${courage} reasons to keep going.
*page_break Keep climbing
*finish
